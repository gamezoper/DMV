🔒 Privacy First Architecture

Game Zoper values and respects your personal privacy. DMV Permit Practice Test DOES NOT require account registration, login credentials, email addresses, or phone numbers to use. 100% of your study activity, test scores, bookmarks, and learning progress remain stored locally on your personal device.

1. Introduction & Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Game Zoper ("we", "us", or "our") handles user information when you install and use the DMV Permit Practice Test mobile application ("App") available on the Google Play Store. We are committed to operating in full compliance with Google Play Developer Policies, the European General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), and the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

2. Information We DO NOT Collect

To provide a seamless, secure, and friction-free learning experience, our app is engineered so that we do not request, harvest, store, or sell any Personally Identifiable Information (PII). Specifically:

  • No User Account: You do not need to create a profile, log in with Google/Facebook, or provide passwords.
  • No Contact Details: We do not ask for or collect your name, email address, physical address, or phone number.
  • No Financial Data: The application does not collect credit card, debit card, bank, or payment details.
  • No Precise Location Data: We do not track or request GPS or fine/coarse geographical coordinates.
  • No Sensitive Media: The app does not access your camera, photo gallery, microphone, contact list, or external file storage.

3. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

To remember your educational progress across study sessions, the App saves non-sensitive configuration data locally on your device using Android's private SharedPreferences sandbox:

  • State & License Preferences: Your selected US State (e.g., California, Texas, Florida) and vehicle license category (Car, CDL, Motorcycle).
  • Quiz Performance & Scores: Completed quiz scores, percentage accuracy, stars earned, and test completion records.
  • Streak & Activity Timestamps: Daily study streak counter and calendar practice records to motivate regular study.
  • Bookmarked Questions: The list of question identifiers you have flagged for revision.
  • Display & UX Preferences: Dark mode preference and haptic feedback toggles.

Control Over Your Local Data: You have full control over this data. You can erase 100% of your stored progress, bookmarks, and scores at any time by navigating to Settings > Reset All Progress within the App, or by clearing the App's data via your device's Android Application Manager.

4. Third-Party Services & Advertising (Google AdMob)

To keep this educational application free and accessible for all learners, we partner with Google AdMob (Google LLC) to display advertisements. AdMob may automatically process certain technical device signals when requesting or presenting advertisements:

  • Device Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (GAID / AAID), device model, hardware specifications, and operating system version.
  • Approximate Network Info: IP address (used to infer coarse country/region for localized ad delivery).
  • Ad Interaction Analytics: Ad views, clicks, impressions, and anti-fraud telemetry to prevent click spam and invalid traffic.

How Ads are Shown in This App:

  • Interstitial Ads: Shown only on natural cognitive breaks (such as after completing every second practice quiz) with a mandatory 75-second safety cooldown timer to prevent disruptive pop-ups.
  • Rewarded Ads: 100% user opt-in. A rewarded video is displayed ONLY when you explicitly choose to watch one in order to unlock the Question Review breakdown.

Opting Out of Personalized Advertising:
You can easily control, reset, or disable personalized ad tracking at any time through your Android device settings:
Settings > Google > Ads > Delete advertising ID (or Opt out of Ads Personalization).

5. Android Permissions Used

The application requests only the minimum essential technical permissions needed to run:

Android Permission Category Technical Purpose
android.permission.INTERNET Network Required to load advertisements via Google Mobile Ads SDK and open external support/feedback links.
android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Network Enables the app to verify whether active internet connectivity exists before attempting to load video ads.
android.permission.VIBRATE Hardware Provides subtle tactile haptic feedback when tapping correct or incorrect quiz answers (can be toggled in Settings).
com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID Advertising Standard Google Play Services permission required on Android 12+ for AdMob advertising identifier support.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Our services are designed for teenagers and adults (aged 15 to 16 and older) preparing for their official learner's permit and driver's license exams. We do not direct our services to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us immediately, and we will take prompt corrective action.

7. Your Privacy Rights (GDPR & CCPA/CPRA)

Depending on your location (such as the European Economic Area or the State of California), you are entitled to specific rights under data protection laws:

  • Right to Know / Access: You have the right to know what data is collected. As noted, all quiz progress is stored strictly on your local device.
  • Right to Deletion: You can wipe all stored data at any time via the "Reset All Progress" button in Settings.
  • Right to Opt-Out: We do NOT sell or share your personal data to data brokers. You can opt out of personalized ads via Android settings.
  • Non-Discrimination: We do not discriminate against users who exercise their statutory privacy rights.

8. Data Security & Storage

We employ industry-standard technical safeguards. Proprietary questions and vehicle mappings bundled within the application are protected using AES-256 binary encryption. Because user study metrics are saved locally within the protected application sandbox of your operating system, they cannot be accessed by unauthorized third-party apps.
